I am Kecheng Huang (黄克成), a Tenure-Track Professor (Teli Young Fellow) at [Beijing Institute of Technology, Zhuhai](https://zh.bit.edu.cn/) in [Computing Science Group](https://ai.bitzh.edu.cn/cs/), Aerospace and Informatics Domain. I am currently seeking candidates for several positions, including Postdoc, PhD, Master, and Undergraduate Interns. My research interests encompass novel computer architectures, system designs, databases and AI4Sys & Sys4AI. For inquiries or collaborations, please feel free to reach out to me at hkc@bit.edu.cn.

I was a post-doctoral fellow in the CSE Department at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), under the supervision of [Prof. Zili Shao]((http://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~shao/)). I obtained my PhD degree at CUHK in 2025, also guided by Prof. Shao. During my doctoral studies, I engaged in sustained research collaborations with both academia ([Prof. Feng Chen at IUB](https://homes.luddy.indiana.edu/fchen25/) and [Prof. Tong Zhang at RPI](https://sites.ecse.rpi.edu/~tzhang/)), and industry partners ([Huawei Hong Kong Research Center](https://career.huawei.com/reccampportal/hk/portal/huaweilife.html) and [Huawei Storage Product Line](https://e.huawei.com/cn/products/storage)).
